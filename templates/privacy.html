Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and account credentials when you register. We also collect the expense data you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help operate and secure the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide the core functionality of Spendly: storing your expenses, generating spending summaries, and letting you sign in to your account. We may also use your email address to send you important account or service-related notices. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure. If you delete your account, we will remove your data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it for legal reasons.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) solely to operate the Service, and these providers only process data on our behalf under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page, and we will be happy to help.
